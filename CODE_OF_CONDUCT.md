# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone who works on or uses it,
regardless of experience, background, identity or location.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and constructive; critique ideas, not people.
* Assume good faith and be patient with newcomers.
* Credit other people's work.
* Keep discussion focused on improving the template.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
* Publishing someone else's private information.
* Posting real client data, credentials, vulnerabilities in third-party systems, or anything that could harm others.
* Spam or promotional content unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that violate this Code and may restrict participation
for repeated or serious violations. To report a concern, contact the maintainer through the details on their
GitHub profile, or open a private security advisory in this repository.

## Scope

This Code applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to public spaces
when someone is representing the project.
