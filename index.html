Vendor Risk Assessment (TPRM)

Tiering · 60-question questionnaire · residual rating · contract clauses · dashboard. Aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 A.5.19–A.5.23, NIST CSF 2.0 GV.SC, SOC 2 CC9.2 and ISO/IEC 42001 A.10. Nothing leaves your browser.

Fictional sample data. This demo opens on six fictional vendors and a worked assessment so you can see what the results look like.

How it works

1 Inventory: list your vendors and pick four risk factors; the inherent score and tier are computed. 2 Questionnaire: select a vendor and enter the answers; domain scores, overall score, band and residual rating update as you go. 3 Contract: tick the clauses present in the signed contract; missing required clauses are counted per tier. 4 Dashboard: vendors by tier and rating, Tier 1 coverage, overdue reviews. A draft is kept in this browser; export JSON to keep a dated record. The same model drives the Excel workbook in this repository.
